Scratch Quiz!

My Scratch quiz

More Information

This quiz tests you on random bits of scratch trivia! I dont think it should be too hard because there are only 5 questions. I decided to make my project about Scratch and its features while also showing the player it's features. I included many sprites, a forever loop, a variable, and more. Yes.

How to play: you click the buttons to get through the quiz. Every time you pick the wrong answer you lose a life and when you pick the right answer you gain a life!

Programming structures I used

I mainly used a lot of when sprite clicked blocks for when you click the buttons but I also used a lot of if blocks for checking to see if the button you clicked was the right one. I used a forever loop to check if the player was on the last slide of the game and I also added the correct answers to a list in the background corresponding to the button that you click to answer it (1 is upper left, 2 is upper right, 3 is bottom left and 4 is bottom right.) so for example if you pressed the top left answer it would check to see based off of the question number that you on if 1 is the correct answer to the question.
